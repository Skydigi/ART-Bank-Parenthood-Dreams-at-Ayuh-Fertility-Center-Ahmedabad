## 1. Overview

- The ART Bank helps patients facing low egg reserve, poor sperm quality, genetic disorders, advanced maternal age or other fertility problems.
- All donors are screened for general health, genetic conditions and infectious diseases before being accepted.
- Patients can also freeze their own eggs or sperm for later use.

---

## 2. What Is an ART Bank?

- A facility that screens, collects, registers and freezes donated sperm and eggs for use in **IVF, ICSI and IUI**.
- Follows ICMR guidelines and uses vitrification-style freezing, so samples can stay usable for years.
- **Who it serves:**
  - Couples
  - Single parents
  - Same-sex couples
  - People preserving fertility before treatment such as chemotherapy
- Donors stay anonymous, and donors are matched to each recipient.

## 3. Responsibilities & Regulations

| Area | What the page says |
|---|---|
| Registration | Registered with the National Registry of ART Banks (NRAB); licensed by the National ART Authority (NAA) |
| Legal framework | Follows the ART (Regulation) Act, 2021 |
| Donor age limits | Men: 21–55 years · Women: 23–35 years |
| Mandatory tests | HIV, HBsAg (Hepatitis B), VDRL (Syphilis), HCV (Hepatitis C) |
| Record keeping | Donor records kept confidential; reported digitally to the National Registry within 1 month of enrolment |
| Storage period | Up to 10 years; after that, discarded or used for research with consent |
| Data sharing | Donor details shared only with the National Registry or under a court order |
| Complaints | A Grievance Cell handles donor and recipient concerns |

## 5. FAQs

**What is an ART Bank?**
A licensed facility that stores donor eggs and sperm for IVF/ICSI, and freezes eggs, sperm or embryos.

**Who can benefit?**
People with low ovarian reserve, poor sperm quality, genetic disorders or repeated IVF failure, as well as single parents and same-sex couples.

**Is it safe and legal?**
Yes. Using donor eggs and sperm is regulated under the ART Act 2021, and donors go through strict screening.

**Is my privacy protected?**
Donors stay anonymous, and recipient details aren't shared.

**Can I freeze my eggs, sperm or embryos?**
Yes. For example, before chemotherapy or to delay parenthood.
